About Velora Lens Studio

Founded in 2017, Velora Lens Studio was born from a deep passion for storytelling through the lens. What started as a solo photographer's dream in Lahore has grown into one of Pakistan's most respected luxury wedding photography and videography studios.

We believe that every wedding, every couple, every family carries a story that deserves to be told with beauty, honesty, and artistic excellence. Our approach blends the warmth of traditional Pakistani wedding culture with the elegance of modern cinematic photography.

With a team of expert photographers, cinematographers, and drone operators, we cover every detail — from the golden glow of a mehndi evening to the grandeur of a walima reception — ensuring that not a single precious moment goes uncaptured.
