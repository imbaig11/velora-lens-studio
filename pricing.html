Questions

Common Questions

Do you travel outside Lahore?
Yes! We cover all major cities of Pakistan including Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Multan, Faisalabad, and more. Travel charges may apply depending on distance.
How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 2-3 months in advance, especially for peak wedding season (October–February). Popular dates fill up quickly!
What is your delivery timeline?
Edited photos are delivered within 3–7 business days. Cinematic videos take 2–4 weeks depending on length and complexity. Rush delivery available at extra cost.
Can I customize a package?
Absolutely! Contact us through the booking form and describe your needs. We'll create a custom package that fits your event and budget perfectly.
Is a deposit required to confirm booking?
Yes, a 30% advance deposit is required to confirm and lock in your date. The remaining balance is due on or before the event day.
